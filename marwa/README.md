# 💖 Happy Birthday Marwa — Romantic Interactive Website

A romantic, interactive birthday surprise website dedicated to **Marwa**, built with HTML5, CSS3, and modern JavaScript.

---

## ✨ Features & Journey

1. **Stage 1 — Welcome & Direct Unlock**:
   - Romantic animated background with floating hearts, ambient glow, and sparkling particles.
   - Elegant header: *"A Special Surprise For Someone Special ❤️"*
   - Single-click glowing button: **"Open Your Surprise ✨❤️"** (no secret code needed).
   - Smooth magical transition upon opening with celebration confetti and auto-playing romantic music.

2. **Stage 2 — Progressive Hand-Drawn Glowing Heart**:
   - Midnight romantic dark violet background with floating stardust.
   - A **huge heart drawn dynamically as if by hand**, using progressive SVG stroke drawing with a luminous pen spark tip emitting glittering particles.
   - Smooth heartbeat pulse once the heart finishes drawing.
   - Grand birthday greeting appears with golden-rose shimmer:
     **`HAPPY BIRTHDAY MARWA ❤️`**
   - Romantic background melody (*Chopin - Nocturne Op. 9 No. 2*) with floating mute/unmute control widget and Web Audio API fallback.

3. **Stage 3 — Her Memories & Birthday Wishes Gallery**:
   - Custom cards showcasing Marwa's photos with glassmorphism, soft glowing bevels, and floating badges.
   - Personalized romantic English birthday messages:
     - **Message 1:** *"Happy Birthday, Marwa! ❤️ Today is your special day, and I hope it brings you endless happiness, beautiful memories, and everything your heart wishes for."*
     - **Message 2:** *"You deserve all the beautiful things in this world. Never stop smiling, because your smile makes everything brighter."*
     - **Message 3:** *"May every new year of your life bring you closer to your dreams. Stay beautiful, stay happy, and always be yourself."*
     - **Final Wish:** *"Happy Birthday once again, Marwa! ❤️ I hope this little surprise makes your day as special as you are. You deserve the best, today and always."*
   - Interactive navigation: previous/next arrows, dot indicators, keyboard arrow controls, and mobile touch swipe gestures.

4. **Grand Finale — "One More Surprise ❤️"**:
   - Interactive love letter modal with warm wax-sealed envelope animation.
   - Multi-burst romantic confetti cannon.
   - Replay button allowing Marwa to re-experience the surprise anytime.

## 🚀 How to Run the Website

### Option 1: Direct Double Click
Simply double-click **`index.html`** in this folder to open it directly in any browser (Chrome, Edge, Safari, Firefox).

### Option 2: Local Web Server
You can also launch a local web server:
```powershell
python -m http.server 8080
```
Then visit: `http://localhost:8080` in your web browser.

## 📸 Replacing or Adding Photos
Marwa's photos are neatly organized in the `assets/images/` folder:
- `assets/images/marwa-1.jpg`
- `assets/images/marwa-2.jpg`
- `assets/images/marwa-3.jpg`

To replace any photo, simply swap the file with the same name, or update the `src` attribute in `index.html`.
